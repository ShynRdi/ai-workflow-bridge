# Code of Conduct

We want AI Workflow Bridge to be a useful, technically rigorous, and welcoming open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing technical disagreements.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith while still taking security and privacy concerns seriously.
- Respect users' private data, credentials, prompts, logs, and account information.
- Give credit for contributions and prior work.
- Keep issue discussions relevant and actionable.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, publishing another person's credentials/private data, deliberate disruption, spam, or attempts to pressure contributors into unsafe security shortcuts are not acceptable.

## Security discussions

Do not post working exploits, credentials, session data, or sensitive reproduction material in public issues. Follow `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users. Serious concerns should be raised through the repository's private/security contact mechanisms where possible.
